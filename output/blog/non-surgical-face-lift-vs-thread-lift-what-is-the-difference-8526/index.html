You want a fresher, lifted look without going under the knife, but the choices are confusing. A “non-surgical face lift” usually means a package of injectables and skin treatments designed to lift, tighten and restore lost volume. A thread lift is a different tool: it places dissolvable sutures to support and reposition tissue. Which is better depends on your anatomy, how much sagging there is, and your recovery goals. Below I explain what each treatment can realistically do, who might suit each option, and why a detailed consultation matters.

Non-Surgical Face Lift vs Thread Lift: Key Differences, What People Mean By A Non-Surgical Face Lift

Most clients use the phrase to describe a combination approach rather than one single treatment. It commonly includes dermal filler to restore lost volume, anti-wrinkle injections to relax muscle pull, and skin treatments to improve texture and tighten. In our clinic a full relift package can use up to 20 syringes of filler depending on the bespoke plan, plus a full face of anti-wrinkle treatment including DAO and lips when appropriate. That package typically takes around three hours and gives major, immediately visible change.
Important to be clear: non-surgical facelifts do not remove skin. They replace volume and reposition soft tissue by supporting the face from underneath. Results and price vary with the amount of filler needed, which your practitioner decides during consultation.

How A Thread Lift Works

A thread lift uses dissolvable sutures placed under local anaesthetic to physically lift and support drooping tissue. Threads can give a visible mechanical lift to the mid-face, jawline or neck depending on placement. At The Gorgeous Clinic threads are offered at a fixed price that includes local anaesthetic and a compression garment when needed. The number of threads used is up to the practitioner and client, but the price does not change with thread count.
Threads are best when there is mild to moderate sagging and when a structural lift is needed alongside volume replacement. They do not cut away excess skin and they are not a substitute for surgical facelifts in cases of significant skin laxity. Recovery is usually shorter than surgery, but some swelling and bruising can occur.

Which Suits You: Threads Or Non-Surgical Plan

Choice depends on your face, how much tissue has descended, and the underlying cause of the look you dislike. If volume loss is the main issue, layers of strategically placed filler can restore youthful contours without tightening skin directly. If soft tissue has dropped and you want mechanical lift, threads can provide added support.
Our advanced practitioners always assess the anatomy. We do not simply fill the shadow. A dropped brow may be temple volume loss, not brow muscle alone. Naso-labial folds often come from changes in the pyriform fossa, so we rarely place cannula filler directly into the fold if it could disturb the smile. The best plan is usually a combination tailored to the real anatomical cause.

What To Expect At Consultation And Treatment

Consultation is where the decision is made. Expect an anatomical assessment, photographs and a discussion of realistic outcomes and downtime. We will explain how many syringes might be needed for a non-surgical relift, and why that number can vary. For threads we will explain technique, thread type, expected discomfort and aftercare including the supplied compression garment.
If you proceed on the day, a full non-surgical relift can take about three hours. Thread lifts are usually shorter but depend on the areas treated. Both treatments involve temporary swelling and possible bruising. A careful consultation also reviews medical history and ensures your treatment plan reduces risk and respects facial balance.

Aftercare, Longevity And Choosing A Clinic

Aftercare differs by treatment. Fillers settle over weeks while anti-wrinkle injections take about two weeks to show full effect. Thread lift results are often visible immediately and refine as swelling settles. Expect follow-up checks, temporary lifestyle restrictions, and guidance on sun protection and skincare to protect your investment.
Choosing a clinic should be based on practitioner anatomy knowledge, documented safety standards, and clear pricing. At The Gorgeous Clinic our advanced team works anatomically, avoids filling shadows without cause, and explains why a particular technique suits your face.

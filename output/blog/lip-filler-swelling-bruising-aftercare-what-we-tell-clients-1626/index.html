Lip Filler: Swelling, Bruising and Aftercare, What We Tell Clients, What happens straight after

Immediately after dermal filler lips you will usually see obvious swelling and mild redness. This is the body reacting to the tiny injections and the hyaluronic acid filler holding water. We always explain that lips often look fuller and a little uneven at first; this is normal and usually improves as swelling settles.
What matters most in the first few days is protecting the area from pressure and heat, and avoiding anything that increases blood flow. Expect tenderness and a slight firmness where product was placed. These signs are part of normal lip filler recovery and do not necessarily mean there is a problem. If you have severe pain, spreading redness or a fever, contact us straight away.

Swelling, bruising and lumps

Lip filler swelling is the most common early effect and is usually worst in the first 48 to 72 hours. Bruising can appear around injection points and may be purple, blue or yellow as it heals. Small nodules or firmness can be felt at first: often these are temporary areas of product or local swelling and soften during lip filler healing.
Lumps that do not reduce after two weeks, or are painful, red or warm, should be assessed. One thing clients are often surprised by is temporary asymmetry while tissues settle; the side with less swelling may look smaller for a few days. If you have concerns, call us rather than waiting in worry, especially if you notice increasing pain, spreading redness or any signs of infection.[1]

Practical aftercare do's and don'ts

What not to do after lip filler matters more than complicated routines. Avoid heavy pressure on your lips for a month: no tight masks, no aggressive lip rubbing and avoid sleeping face-down. Skip saunas, hot tubs and prolonged hot showers for 48 to 72 hours, because heat increases swelling and may prolong recovery. Limit alcohol for 24 to 48 hours as it increases bruising and blood flow.
Light walking is fine but avoid strenuous exercise that makes you hot for the first two days. Wait 24 hours before applying lipstick or heavy makeup directly on injection points. Avoid kissing or intimate mouth contact for 24 to 48 hours. If shown in clinic, gentle lip filler massage is only done if the clinician advises it.

Healing timeline and when lips settle

Every person heals differently, so we avoid promising exact healing times. Most people see the worst swelling in days 1 to 3 and then gradual improvement over two weeks. By 2 to 4 weeks lips usually feel softer and more settled, and final lip filler results become clearer. If additional correction is needed, we often review at around two weeks or four weeks to see how tissues have settled.
One important point we always explain: early changes are common, and minor asymmetry often resolves without intervention. If you are worried that something is not settling, contact us for a clinical review rather than relying on online photos or forums.

Follow-up and choosing a clinic

We always tell clients that a clear plan and follow-up are part of safe lip enhancement. Free consultations let us explain risks, expected aftercare and the clinic's emergency contact process. If you are local to Blackpool or the Fylde Coast, choose a clinic where you can get a prompt review if needed. Good clinics document the product used and offer a review appointment to check lip filler recovery.
If you notice worsening pain, spreading redness, persistent lumps beyond two weeks, or any other worrying signs, contact your clinic for assessment. If you would like advice on what would suit your face, The Gorgeous Clinic can talk you through your options.

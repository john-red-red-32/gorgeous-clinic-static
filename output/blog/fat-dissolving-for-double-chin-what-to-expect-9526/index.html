If a soft jawline or small double chin is what bothers you, fat-dissolving injections can be a realistic option. Many people expect an instant shrink, but results take time and depend on what is causing the fullness. A careful assessment matters. We check skin laxity, fat distribution, weight changes and jawline structure to decide if injections, filler, skin tightening or a combination will help most. Below is what to expect from treatment, typical timelines, and when another approach may be better.

Fat Dissolving for Double Chin: What To Expect, What Causes A Double Chin?

A noticeable double chin can come from several places: extra fat under the chin (submental fat), loose or sagging skin, muscle banding, or a combination. Genetics and weight both matter, but so do ageing changes in skin and bone that alter the jawline. We always separate volume from laxity during assessment because the treatment is different for each cause.  
Clinically, fat under the chin can be localised even in people of normal weight. If skin is loose, removing some fat will not restore a sharp jawline on its own. That is why we often discuss complementary options such as skin tightening or subtle jawline filler to define the lower face.

How Fat-Dissolving Injections Work

Fat-dissolving injections contain a substance that breaks down fat cells in the treated area. The practitioner injects small amounts across the submental area to target pockets of fat. This is a local treatment, not a weight-loss injection. It reduces local fat volume by causing gradual removal of the treated fat by the body’s natural processes.
Expect the area to swell after treatment. The process is gradual: you may spot early changes at 4 to 8 weeks, but the full effect usually appears around 12 to 16 weeks. Most people need between one and four sessions for a chin area, spaced several weeks apart depending on how much reduction is needed and how you respond.

What To Expect After Treatment

Swelling is the most common immediate effect and can be quite pronounced for the first 7 to 14 days. That is normal and part of the way the body clears the treated fat. Swelling typically reduces substantially by week 4, though mild firmness or nodularity may persist longer. Because volume loss is slow, you may not notice visible change while swelling is masking it.
A useful way to check progress is to look at the jawline edges, rather than expecting the central area to suddenly disappear. Think of a balloon slowly deflating: the sides where the material is thinner alter first, so you often see reduction nearest the jawline before the middle clears.

Who Suits This Treatment?

Fat-dissolving injections suit people with a local pocket of submental fat and reasonable skin elasticity. They are less useful when excess skin, significant laxity, or poor muscle tone are the main problem. If you have lost a lot of weight and the skin hangs, skin tightening or a surgical approach may give better results.
Assessment matters because we choose the safest, most effective option for your anatomy. At consultation we check how much fat is present, how elastic the skin is, and whether other treatments such as jawline filler or radiofrequency skin tightening would help instead or alongside injections.

Choosing Treatment And Timelines

A typical course is one to four sessions for the chin, with results building over weeks and finishing around 12 to 16 weeks after the final session. If you need a sharper jawline, combining modest jawline filler or a skin-tightening treatment can be more effective than relying on fat reduction alone. Weight changes also affect submental fat, so maintaining a stable weight helps keep results.
Safety and realistic expectations are the priority. If you are considering treatment, a consultation is the safest place to start. The Gorgeous Clinic can explain which approach is likely to give the look you want while keeping your features balanced and natural.

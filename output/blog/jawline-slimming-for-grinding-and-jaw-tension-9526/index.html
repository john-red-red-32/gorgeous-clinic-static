You may grind your teeth, wake with jaw soreness, or feel your lower face looks wider than you would like. Carefully placed anti-wrinkle injections can reduce masseter muscle activity in suitable people, easing teeth grinding and jaw tension, and sometimes softening a heavy-looking lower face. This is not a one-size-fits-all slimming trend. Facial balance, bite mechanics and how much the muscle contributes to your jaw shape all matter. Read what usually happens, who might benefit, how results develop, and why experienced judgement matters.

Jawline Slimming For Grinding And Jaw Tension, What the treatment does

The treatment uses anti-wrinkle injections to reduce activity in the masseter muscle, the large chewing muscle at the angle of the jaw. When the masseter is overactive from clenching or grinding, injections can relax it, which often eases jaw pain, reduces teeth-grinding forces and can make the lower face look softer over months.
This is not a fat-reducing treatment. The change comes from reduced muscle bulk, not from removing fat. If a broad jaw is mostly due to bone structure or fat, injections will not give a slimmed look. An experienced clinician will check how much the muscle contributes to your jaw width before recommending treatment. Evidence and clinical experience show benefit for bruxism and masseter-related jaw pain in selected patients [1].

Who might benefit

Typical reasons people ask about this are teeth grinding, waking with jaw ache, chronic jaw tension, or wanting a less square-looking lower face. It suits people whose bite is stable and whose broad jaw comes largely from a prominent masseter muscle rather than bone or fat.
It may not be suitable if you have dental issues, loose teeth, unmanaged sleep apnoea, or if your bite requires dental or orthodontic care first. We always check dental history and, where appropriate, suggest speaking to your dentist. A careful assessment of facial proportions is essential because reducing the muscle can change how the chin and lower face relate to the rest of your face.
1

What to expect after treatment

You may notice reduced jaw tension and less grinding within two to four weeks. Muscle thinning and a softer jawline take longer, typically three to four months, because muscle bulk reduces gradually. Early relief for pain or discomfort often precedes visible slimming. The full cosmetic effect may be clearer at three to six months.
Results vary. Many patients see meaningful improvement for four to nine months, with repeat treatment usually needed to maintain effect. Longevity depends on dose, how active your muscles are, and your individual metabolism. We take photos and measure progress so any repeat treatment is properly judged against how you responded previously.
1
NICE - Botulinum toxin and other neuromodulators - NICE.org.uk - 2019

Risks, realistic expectations

Common side effects are mild bruising, temporary weakness when chewing, and short-term soreness at injection sites. Rarely, injections can affect nearby muscles and change smile symmetry or cause temporary dysphagia. These risks are reduced by experienced technique and correct dosing.
Have realistic expectations. If bone or fat gives a square jaw, injections alone will not produce dramatic slimming. Sometimes adjunctive approaches such as dental treatment, physiotherapy, or targeted fat-reduction are needed. An honest consultation discusses likely benefit, possible need for repeat treatments, and measurable goals tailored to your face.
1
MHRA - Safety information on botulinum toxin - GOV.UK - 2020

Choosing a practitioner

Choose a clinician experienced in treating the masseter, who understands facial proportions and works with your dentist when needed. Ask about training, complication management and whether they measure muscle activity or use photos to track results. A good consultation explores dental history, sleep, clenching habits and what a successful outcome looks like.

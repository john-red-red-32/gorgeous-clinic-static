When Do Anti-Wrinkle Injections Start Working?

You want to look fresher, not frozen, and you are asking a sensible question: how long until anti-wrinkle injections actually work? Most people notice a gentle softening within days, with fuller effect arriving over about two weeks. This piece explains the typical timeline, why the change is gradual, what we check at review, and when small tweaks may be needed. It is aimed at people considering treatment around Blackpool and the Fylde Coast who want realistic expectations and a steady, safe approach.

When Do Anti-Wrinkle Injections Start Working?, What the treatment does

Anti-wrinkle injections work by temporarily reducing activity in small facial muscles that cause dynamic lines. That means frown lines, forehead lines and crow's feet stop creasing so visibly when you make expressions. The goal is softer movement, not a frozen face.
Because the product changes muscle activity rather than instantly smoothing skin, you will not see a blanket, immediate wrinkle-free look. Instead, look for a progressive reduction in line depth as the muscles relax over days and weeks.

Early signs: days 1 to 4

In the first 24 to 48 hours you may notice initial softening. Many clients tell us they feel a slight change when frowning or laughing. This stage is often subtle and variable between people.
Mild swelling, tiny red pinpricks or local tenderness can occur for 48 hours. These do not affect how the product works. Avoid strenuous exercise, alcohol and blood-thinning medications for 24 to 48 hours unless your prescriber advised otherwise, to reduce bruising risk.

The full effect: days 5 to 14

Most of the visible change happens between days five and fourteen. Muscle activity continues to reduce during this window, and lines become noticeably less pronounced. By day fourteen the majority of clients see their planned result.
Clinics commonly book a review around two weeks. This lets your practitioner assess the balance, correct small asymmetries and decide if a top-up is appropriate. A gentle tweak at this point is normal and responsible rather than an indication of poor technique.
1
British Association of Dermatologists - Patient information on botulinum toxin (https://www.bad.org.uk) - 2023

Why results take time and asymmetry can appear

The product works at a neuromuscular level, blocking signals that tell muscles to contract. Biological processes such as nerve terminal uptake and muscle response need time. Individual anatomy, previous treatments and muscle strength all affect the pace and degree of change.
Some asymmetry is common while results settle. One side of the face may relax sooner than the other. That is why review appointments exist: to make small, targeted adjustments and ensure a balanced, natural outcome.

Aftercare, reviews and choosing a clinic

After treatment, allow two weeks before judging the final result. Keep a simple aftercare routine: avoid rubbing or massaging treated areas for 24 hours, skip intensive facial treatments for one to two weeks, and protect skin from direct sun. If you have concerns between appointment and review, contact your clinic sooner.

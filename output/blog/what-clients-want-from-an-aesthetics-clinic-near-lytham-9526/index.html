What a proper consultation includes

A thorough consultation is not a sales pitch. Expect a medical history review, a facial analysis in different lights and an honest discussion of risks and realistic outcomes. Practitioners should document goals, propose options and explain why they might recommend doing less or delaying treatment.
Good clinics use photos, measurements and sometimes facial-mapping tools to plan treatments. You should leave with a written plan, aftercare details, and a clear follow-up timeline. Free consultations remove pressure and let you ask questions without commitment. If a practitioner rushes you or avoids discussing complications, consider walking away.

Safety, reviews and aftercare

Safety is about systems, not just qualifications. Look for visible infection-control procedures, clear consent paperwork and established emergency protocols. Check that a qualified prescriber assesses you and that follow-up is included. Reading many recent reviews gives a clearer picture than a single glowing testimonial.
Aftercare matters as much as the procedure. Good clinics provide written aftercare, accessible contact routes for concerns, and scheduled reviews so complications are picked up early. National guidance and professional bodies support these expectations. If aftercare feels informal or hard to access, consider another clinic. [1] [2]

Common treatments clients choose

Clients who want natural, ageing-appropriate improvements most commonly choose conservative anti-wrinkle injections for dynamic lines, subtle dermal filler for volume support, and skin-quality treatments such as medical peels, microneedling or collagen stimulators. These options can be combined over time to maintain a refreshed appearance without dramatic change.
Discussion should always cover timing, recovery and realistic timelines for results. Skin treatments often need a course spaced over months. Fillers wear down gradually and require maintenance. Expect clear cost estimates, a plan for follow-up, and honest answers if a different approach would be safer or more effective.

You want fresher, firmer skin without obvious filler lumps or an over-treated look. Profhilo is often described as an injectable moisturiser rather than a filler, and many clients ask what it can realistically do for skin texture, hydration and subtle lift. This piece explains how Profhilo works, when you see results, who might suit it, and how it compares with other skin-quality options we commonly use, such as polynucleotides, INFINI Aquabooster and collagen induction therapy.

How Profhilo Works For Skin Hydration And Glow, What Profhilo Is

Profhilo is a high-concentration hyaluronic acid (HA) product designed to improve skin quality rather than to add volume. It is not a dermal filler; it spreads within the superficial subdermal layer to increase hydration, improve skin texture and create a subtle tightening effect. The product’s low-modified HA allows it to bio-remodel skin by encouraging hydration and supporting the extracellular matrix.
Patients often describe a ‘glow’ and softer texture rather than a lifted, filled look. Because Profhilo works by improving the skin environment, it is most useful for early to moderate skin laxity and for anyone who wants healthier-looking skin without obvious contour changes.

How The Treatment Works

Treatment uses a limited injection pattern, usually five microinjections per side of the face, where small boluses of HA are placed into the superficial subdermal plane. The material spreads and attracts water, increasing hydration and tissue turgor over several weeks. A typical course is two treatments spaced four weeks apart, then maintenance at six to twelve months depending on response.
Results usually begin to appear around three to four weeks after the first session and become more apparent after the second. Expect skin to feel smoother, look more radiant and have improved firmness. The technique is deliberately conservative; it is not a substitute for targeted volumising or deep collagen stimulation.

Who Might Suit Profhilo

Profhilo is suitable for people noticing dullness, mild loss of firmness, fine lines and dehydration. It suits clients in their 30s to 60s who want a natural improvement rather than structural change. It is often chosen by those who dislike the idea of traditional fillers but want better skin quality.
Profhilo is not the best choice when you need targeted volume replacement, such as restoring cheek or chin projection. If you already have moderate to advanced laxity, we commonly recommend combining or choosing alternative treatments that offer stronger collagen induction or deeper lift.

How It Compares With Other Options

Compared with polynucleotides and other skin boosters, Profhilo’s effect is mainly hydrating and bio-remodelling. Polynucleotide products aim to stimulate cellular activity and can feel more regenerative in some studies. Skin boosters such as INFINI Aquabooster or Jalupro have lower viscosity and can be easier to distribute for certain areas, making them more versatile for fine lines and under-eye work.
Collagen induction therapy (microneedling with growth factors or PRP) creates a different outcome. A single Profhilo session rarely matches the structural collagen increase that a course of CIT can produce. For clients wanting deeper, longer-lasting collagen stimulation, we often prefer CIT or combining Profhilo with microneedling depending on the aesthetic goal.

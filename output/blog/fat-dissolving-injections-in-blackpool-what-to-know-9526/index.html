Fat Dissolving Injections in Blackpool: What to Know, What Aqualyx Is

Aqualyx is a deoxycholic acid based solution designed to break down small, localised fat deposits. It acts by disrupting fat cell walls so the body can gradually remove the contents through normal metabolic processes. We use Aqualyx because it has an established clinical history and practical experience supporting its use for facial pockets such as under the chin and some body areas.
We do not use newer, unproven products often promoted on social media, for example the so-called \"lemon bottle\", because there is limited independent clinical evidence for their safety and effectiveness. A careful product choice reduces avoidable risk and gives steadier, predictable results.

Where It Works Best

Aqualyx is most effective for small, discrete pockets of fat. Common facial areas are the submental area (under the chin), jowls and superficial fat that sits above or lateral to the nasolabial fold when superficial fat migration has occurred. On the body it can help selected, small areas such as a kettle handle or a stubborn roll, but it is not a solution for general weight loss.
You should be near your target weight to get the best result for body pockets. The treatment is a spot reduction method, not a substitute for surgical liposuction. A realistic expectation is a softer, noticeable reduction rather than dramatic surgical change.

What To Expect After Treatment

Expect swelling and firmness where the product is injected. Swelling is common and usually most noticeable in the first 48 to 72 hours, then gradually reduces but can persist as mild-to-moderate swelling for around two weeks. Tenderness and temporary numbness are possible. The treated area may feel firm or lumpy as the body clears disrupted fat cells.
Most people see gradual change. Final results are typically assessed at 12 to 16 weeks. If you need more reduction, retreatment can be considered from five weeks after the previous session, depending on your healing and goals. We will explain expected downtime at consultation.

Sessions, Results And Limits

How many sessions you need varies by area and your goals. For the chin most people need between one and four sessions. Each session addresses a modest volume of fat. Body treatments are far more variable and may need more sessions or may not be the best option compared with other techniques. We assess each case individually at a consultation.
This is not surgical liposuction. Surgical options remove larger volumes of fat and reshape the area more dramatically. Fat dissolving injections give softer, natural-looking reductions and work well when a subtle correction is the aim. We will be realistic about likely outcomes during a free consultation.

Aftercare And Choosing A Clinic

After treatment avoid strenuous exercise for 24 to 48 hours and skip alcohol for 48 hours to reduce swelling. Gentle massage is sometimes advised for body areas, but only if your clinician recommends it and shows you the correct technique. Follow-up appointments give a chance to check healing and decide if further sessions are appropriate.

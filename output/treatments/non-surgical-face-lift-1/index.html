Ready to look fresher, more lifted, and more like yourself again — without surgery?
Our Non-Surgical Face Lift is a highly advanced full-face rejuvenation treatment designed for clients who want to restore what time has softened, shifted, or hollowed, while still looking completely natural. This is not about looking different. It is about looking beautifully well-kept, well-rested, and subtly turned back.
Advanced, but Never Excessive
Using extremely advanced B-tox techniques alongside carefully placed premium dermal filler, we support, lift, and re-volumise the face in a way that feels elegant, bespoke, and anatomically intelligent. The result is a softer, fresher, more energised version of you.
Every treatment is completely individual. Some clients need only gentle support in a few key areas, while others benefit from more comprehensive full-face restoration. Pricing is therefore dynamic and based on the volume of dermal filler required, which your practitioner will assess with you during your consultation.

Over the course of 2–3 hours, the face is carefully restored like a masterpiece in progress.
Exceptional Value

Recovery & Downtime
Recovery is usually very manageable, though it varies from person to person.
There is always a risk of bruising with dermal filler. Some clients get none at all, some may get one or two small bruises, and more significant bruising is less common. Age, natural healing response, and medications such as aspirin or blood thinners can all affect how long bruising lasts.

Some swelling is also normal, especially in the first week. Most clients feel comfortable returning to work within 1–2 days, though the face can continue to settle and refine over the course of a month.
Dermal Filler
Dermal filler is used to restore lost volume, soften lines, and enhance facial structure in a refined, natural way.
The goal is never to overfill — just to subtly refresh and support the features you already have. We’ll always prioritise proportion and harmony.
Natural, Elegant Results
You will still look like yourself — just fresher, lifted, and more refined.
We specialise in natural but noticeable rejuvenation. You will never leave looking heavy, overfilled, or obviously “done”. The goal is always to restore harmony, soften signs of ageing, and bring back support where it has been lost, while keeping your expression and character beautifully intact.

Optimal results are typically seen around 4 weeks after treatment, once the B-tox has fully taken effect and the dermal filler has settled and integrated.

In many areas of the face, we observe dermal filler lasting well over 2 years, depending on placement and individual metabolism.
Facial Balancing and Harmonisation
Facial balancing is about looking at the face as a whole, rather than focusing on one isolated feature in isolation. The most beautiful results usually come from understanding how the lips, chin, cheeks, jawline, and profile all work together.
By treating the face holistically, we can create results that feel more natural, refined, and balanced overall. Sometimes the best outcome does not come from doing more to one area, but from making thoughtful adjustments that bring the entire face into better harmony.

FAQ

How long does the Non-Surgical Face Lift take?
The procedure generally takes around 3 hours. However, at the 3 hour mark, your clinician may ask you to revisit to complete additional work (such as forehead and temples) in a separate procedure at no additional cost. The end result is our goal, not the hours spent.
What products are used?
We use a combination of high quality dermal filler and Brand B'Tox  (not the weaker, cheaper stuff).
High quality filler provides smoothness and longevity, whilst brand B-tox Anti-wrinkle injections provides the safety and longevity which comes with the the brand.
Will the results be natural?
Absolutely. We will tailor the treatment to your face. We don't follow templates or offer "3ml packages", every one of our Non-surgical Face Lift procedures is carried out from a bespoke plan agreed between you and your clinician.
How long will it last?
It all depends on where the dermal fillers are placed, and if you've had dermal fillers before.
For example, dermal fillers routinely last 18-24 months around the cheekbones, but tend to dissolve naturally in 4-9 months in the lips when it's our first time having them done.  

B'Tox generally lasts around 3-6 months, this is just the nature of the product. However, the anti-ageing effects are long lasting.
Is it safe?
Yes. Dermal Fillers and B-tox have been used in aesthetics for many years.
By trusting your face with experienced medical professionals, you can be sure the upmost care and attention will be taken to ensure you are safe and well looked after.

All our clinics are stocked with EpiPens and other emergency supplies in the unlikely event of an adverse reaction. Our practicing clinicians are trained and insured in First Aid / CPR.
Is there a review or top-up appointment?
Yes — we offer an optional free 2-week review, where we assess symmetry, check muscle response and provide a top-up if clinically appropriate.
This ensures your final result is refined and perfectly balanced.

The cost of this appointment and assosiated product is already included in your treatment price.

When treating certian areas like the forehead for the first time, we may build this slowly over 2-3 sessions to ensure we avoid that "heavy" look. This allows us time to see how your muscles react to our high-strength product.

Once we know your muscles, retreatment is often just one session in the future.
Is it reversible?
Whilst we've never been asked to reverse a Non-surgical Face Lift, the dermal fillers themselves are dissolvable using the Hyaluronidase enzyme, provided you are not allergic to it. B'Tox can not be reversed as such, but can be worn away faster if required.
Is the clinic and practitioner experienced in this procedure?
Yes. In fact, we practically invented it! No other clinics locally offer an "Unlimited B-tox and Dermal Filler" package - the main reason we enjoy these is because the clinician enjoys the opportunity to sculpt an entire face, rather than just work on one section alone.
With regards to the practitioner, we would challange anyone to find another practitioner on the Fylde Coast who has carried out more dermal filler and B'tox procedures than the Clinical Director.

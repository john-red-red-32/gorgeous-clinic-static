Injectable Moisturisers & Skin Boosters
Skin boosters and injectable moisturisers deliver deep hydration directly into the skin, improving luminosity and softness from within.
This is about glow and quality — not volume. We’ll determine if this gentle refresh is right for your skin during consultation.  
Skin Booster vs Filler
Skin boosters and filler are both injectable treatments, but they do very different jobs. Filler is used to create shape, structure, and volume, whereas skin boosters are designed to improve hydration, skin quality, and overall freshness.
In simple terms, fillers enhance contour, while skin boosters improve the condition of the skin itself. Choosing the right treatment depends on whether the goal is facial shaping or healthier, glowier skin.

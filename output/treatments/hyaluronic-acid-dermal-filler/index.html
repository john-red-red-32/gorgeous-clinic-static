How It Works
Dermal fillers are composed of hyaluronic acid, a substance naturally found in the body, that adds volume and stimulates collagen production. The treatment involves precise injections into the targeted areas using fine needles or cannulas, allowing for a natural-looking result.
Comfortable Procedure: To ensure maximum comfort, we use local anaesthetic before treatment. The procedure is quick, with minimal discomfort and virtually no downtime.

Tailored to You: Each treatment is customized to suit your facial structure and desired outcome. Our skilled practitioners will assess your needs and carefully administer the filler to enhance your natural beauty.
Why Choose The Gorgeous Clinic for Dermal Fillers?

Dermal Filler
Dermal filler is used to restore lost volume, soften lines, and enhance facial structure in a refined, natural way.
The goal is never to overfill — just to subtly refresh and support the features you already have. We’ll always prioritise proportion and harmony.
Jawline Contouring: Men vs Women
Jawline contouring is not a one-size-fits-all treatment, because masculine and feminine facial structures are shaped very differently. In men, the aim is often to create a stronger, broader, more angular jawline, while in women the focus is usually on refinement, definition, and elegant shape.
The best results come from respecting the natural character of the face rather than applying the same look to everyone. By tailoring the treatment properly, we can enhance structure in a way that feels balanced, flattering, and appropriate to the individual.  
Facial Balancing and Harmonisation
Facial balancing is about looking at the face as a whole, rather than focusing on one isolated feature in isolation. The most beautiful results usually come from understanding how the lips, chin, cheeks, jawline, and profile all work together.
By treating the face holistically, we can create results that feel more natural, refined, and balanced overall. Sometimes the best outcome does not come from doing more to one area, but from making thoughtful adjustments that bring the entire face into better harmony.

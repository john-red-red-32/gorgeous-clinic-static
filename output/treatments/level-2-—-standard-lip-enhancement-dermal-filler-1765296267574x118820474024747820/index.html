Dermal Filler
Dermal filler is used to restore lost volume, soften lines, and enhance facial structure in a refined, natural way.
The goal is never to overfill — just to subtly refresh and support the features you already have. We’ll always prioritise proportion and harmony.
Why Do Some Lips Look Fake After Filler?
Lips can look unnatural after filler when too much product is used, when the technique is poor, or when the natural shape and boundaries of the lips are not properly respected. It can also happen when volume is placed in the wrong areas, creating a look that feels unbalanced or disconnected from the rest of the face.
The quality of the filler itself also matters, as unsuitable products can sometimes feel firm, uneven, or lumpy. At our clinic, lip enhancement is approached with precision and restraint, so the result looks soft, balanced, and in harmony with your natural features.

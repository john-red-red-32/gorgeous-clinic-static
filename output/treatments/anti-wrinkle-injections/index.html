FAQ

How long does B’tox take to start working?
Most clients begin to see softening of lines around day 6–9, with the full, smooth result visible by day 11–14. This is completely normal — the product activates gradually as it relaxes the targeted muscles.
Which brand of toxin do you use?
We only use brand-name B’tox by Allergan™, the strongest and longest-lasting licensed formulation available in the UK.
We never use cheaper alternatives — your results, safety and longevity always come first.
Will my result look natural?
Yes — when performed correctly.

We analyse your unique muscle patterns rather than following a template, allowing us to soften lines while keeping your expressions as  alive as you'd like them to be.
Does B’tox hurt?
Discomfort is minimal. We use very fine needles and highly controlled technique; most clients describe the sensation as a few tiny scratches. The treatment takes around 10–15 minutes and requires no downtime.
How long do results last?
Most clients enjoy their result for 4–7 months, though this varies based on metabolism and muscle strength.
The product wears off slowly, so a little movement is still a long way from full movement back.

Regular treatment can help the muscles stay more relaxed over time, often extending longevity.
Is there a review or top-up appointment?
Yes — we offer an optional free 2-week review, where we assess symmetry, check muscle response and provide a top-up if clinically appropriate.
This ensures your final result is refined and perfectly balanced.

The cost of this appointment and assosiated product is already included in your treatment price.

When treating certian areas like the forehead for the first time, we may build this slowly over 2-3 sessions to ensure we avoid that "heavy" look. This allows us time to see how your muscles react to our high-strength product.

Once we know your muscles, retreatment is often just one session in the future.
Can B’tox help with facial asymmetry?
Absolutely.
When placed with millimetre precision, B’tox can subtly rebalance uneven brows, smiles or muscle activity — it’s a powerful tool for restoring harmony without surgery.

We routinely treat those who have experienced nerve damage or birth defects to achieve confidence-building symmetry.
How much do you charge for anti-wrinkle injections?
The first area is £200, and each additional area is £50. This includes 24 hour aftercare, the strongest licensed product on the market, and the 2 week review.

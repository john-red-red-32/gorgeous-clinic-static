What is Sculptra / PLLA?
PLLA (Poly-L-Lactic Acid) is a unique, biocompatible substance that stimulates your body's natural collagen production.
Unlike traditional fillers, PLLA offers a gradual, long-term enhancement, focusing on improving skin quality and creating soft, natural-looking volume in areas such as the cheek hollows and temples.

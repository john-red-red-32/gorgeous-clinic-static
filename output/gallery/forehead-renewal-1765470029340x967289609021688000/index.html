Chin Projection & Profile Balancing
©TheGorgeousClinic

Built slowly, beautifully, and completely naturally.

This chin was built carefully over two sessions to achieve a result that looks effortless and completely natural. We used a firmer, structural dermal filler for projection, alongside a small amount of anti-wrinkle treatment to the mentalis muscle to allow the filler to settle undisturbed.
Two weeks after relaxing the muscle, we placed the first syringe and allowed it to integrate fully before refining the result with a second syringe six weeks later. The outcome is a beautifully balanced side profile with soft, elegant projection — nothing artificial, just natural harmony. A subtle lip tweak completed the profile, and she was absolutely delighted with the result.
